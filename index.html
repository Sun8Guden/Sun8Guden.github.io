Guodong SUN | INRIA

 

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the INRIA Centre, Université Côte d’Azur, where my research primarily involves applying stochastic geometry to analyze the performance of wireless communication networks. My current research extends into network information theory, specifically considering the impact of spatial distribution. I am working on this with Samir Perlaza (INRIA), Jean-Marie Gorce (INRIA), and Philippe Mary (INSA de Rennes)
